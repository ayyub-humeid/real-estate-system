Arab Market Marketing (Senior Perspective)

Where to spend first:

1. WhatsApp — not email, not form. Every real-estate deal in the Arab world moves on WhatsApp. Build a
   1-on-1 sales motion: short video demo → WhatsApp follow-up → trial link. This beats any ad.
2. LinkedIn Arabic content — decision-makers (company owners, finance managers) are there in KSA, UAE,
   Jordan, Egypt. 2-3 short Arabic posts/week: "how we helped X manage 50 units", before/after operations
   screenshots. No hard sell.
3. YouTube Arabic tutorials — a 10-minute "how to manage a project in Madar" ranks fast because
   competition is thin in Arabic PropTech. Each tutorial is a demo that never sleeps.
4. Referrals with a reward — the Arab market buys on trust and word of mouth far more than the West. Give
   the first 10 customers a simple deal: bring us one paying customer, get 2 months free. No code needed.

What to avoid early: Google Ads and Meta ads before you have testimonials. You'll burn budget on clicks from people who don't know you and won't pay.
